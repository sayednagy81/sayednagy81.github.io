Loads and preliminary column sizes

Square-column estimates use tributary area under a uniform average load and round up to 5 cm. Structural analysis is required for actual loading, moments, slenderness, code dimensions and reinforcement.

Calculation method and references

Pitch equals the mould base dimension plus bottom rib width. Voids have rectangular bases and straight faces. Presets are model inputs.

Void volume uses the base, midpoint and top areas in Simpson’s rule, which is exact for this geometry. Net concrete equals the gross envelope minus all voids. Self-weight is equivalent thickness times concrete unit weight.

Use for quantity takeoff and preliminary estimates of a rectangular slab. Factored gravity load is the greater of 1.4D and 1.2D + 1.6L. The ACI 318-19 §8.8 screening covers selected geometry criteria for removable forms. ACI 318-19
