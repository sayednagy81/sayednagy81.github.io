Calculation method and references

Pitch equals the mould base dimension plus bottom rib width. Voids have rectangular bases and straight faces. Presets are model inputs.

Void volume uses the base, midpoint and top areas in Simpson’s rule, which is exact for this geometry. Net concrete equals the gross envelope minus all voids. Self-weight is equivalent thickness times concrete unit weight.

Use for quantity takeoff and preliminary estimates of a rectangular slab. Note: how the column loads are calculated

This is an architectural sizing aid, so the detailed loads are built in: slab self-weight from the concrete quantity, finishes 1.5 kN/m², the live load of the chosen use, a 30 cm wide edge beam about span / 10 deep, a 25 cm brick wall (18 kN/m³) up to the beam, and the column self-weight, repeated for every storey. Edge column loads are increased by 25 % and corner loads by 50 % for bending. Capacity uses the short tied-column form σ = 0.35 fcu + 0.67 fy ρ = 11.16 N/mm² (fcu 25, fy 360, ρ 1 %), and no side is less than 30 cm, 1.2 × column height / 15 (short braced column) or the span between columns / 20.

Every alternative has at least the required area and at least the square of the governing minimum side; slim sections (30 cm wide with a tall column) need a structural slenderness check.

Column sections use tributary area under a uniform average load: required area = factored axial load ÷ σ (0.35 fcu + 0.67 fy ρ). Both sides are multiples of 5 cm and at least 30 cm. Structural analysis is required for actual loading, moments, slenderness, punching shear, code dimensions and reinforcement.
